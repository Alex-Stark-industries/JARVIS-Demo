# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first public release of the JARVIS demo — a free, multilingual **chatbot +
advanced voice** desktop app, with the Jarvis interface.

- **Speaks your language** — auto-detects English, Italian, Spanish, French,
  Portuguese or Hindi and replies out loud in it, with a native voice.
- **Chat** with the local model, streamed as he replies.
- **Advanced voice mode** — reactive sphere, hold-Space or tap-to-talk, with neural
  turn detection and barge-in.
- **Five English voices**, saved sessions, and a microphone picker.
- **Fully offline voice**: the speech engine and runtime are bundled.
- Distributed as a per-user Windows installer — no administrator rights needed.

Requires [Ollama](https://ollama.com) running locally with `qwen2.5:3b`.
